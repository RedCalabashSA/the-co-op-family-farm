ABOUT THE CO-OP

Made With Love

Real food, handmade goods, and practical solutions from local makers and our family farm.

The Co-Op is a curated marketplace that connects you with the work of real people — makers, growers and doers — creating quality products that make life better and support stronger communities.

OUR STORY

It Started Around
A Kitchen Table.

The Co-Op began with a simple belief: if somebody around us makes something good, useful and honest, we want to help them find a market.

Some products come from The Family Farm. Others come from friends, neighbours and makers we know personally. The Co-Op brings them together in one curated place.

This is not about filling shelves. It is about real products, practical skills and the people behind them.

WHAT IS THE CO-OP?

A curated marketplace with a human face.

  • Products chosen from makers we know and trust
  • Quality and usefulness before volume
  • Local livelihoods and practical skills kept visible
  • Family Farm products alongside independent makers
