THE WORK

Built to be used.

The Olympus Flower parabolic cooker concentrates sunlight onto the cooking area. Ray’s supplied material says the cookers can boil water, cook soups and stews, bake bread and cake, grill and fry.

The rocket stoves bring the same practical philosophy to efficient wood-fired cooking.
