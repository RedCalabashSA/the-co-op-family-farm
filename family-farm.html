FROM OUR FARM

Our products sit beside everyone else’s.

The Co-Op is not a label we put over every maker. Family Farm goods remain Family Farm goods; Ray remains Ray; Eshelby & Co. remains Eshelby & Co.

The Co-Op is the shared marketplace that brings those products together without erasing where each product comes from.
