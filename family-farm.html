Where The Co-Op Began

The Family Farm is the home base: food, bees, growing, making, practical learning and the relationships that led to the Co-Op.

Our products sit alongside everyone else’s.

The Co-Op is not a label we intend to put over every maker. Family Farm goods remain Family Farm goods; Ray remains Ray; Eshelby & Co. remains Eshelby & Co. The Co-Op is the shared marketplace that brings them together.
