Olympus Flower
Solar Cooker

by Ray

Handmade parabolic solar cooker for clean, sustainable outdoor cooking.

Price on enquiry

When focused towards the sun, the reflector concentrates solar energy onto a pot. Ray’s supplied material says the cooker can boil water, cook soups and stews, bake bread and cake, grill and fry.
